Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a paid Canva subscription?

No. All 50 templates work in the free version of Canva. No paid plan required.

How do I edit the templates?

Download the PPTX file from Gumroad. In Canva, go to Projects → click the + button → Upload files → select the file. Each slide opens as an editable design. Click any text to edit it. Drag your listing photo into the placeholder. Export as PNG and post.

Can I use these on Instagram, Facebook, and Pinterest?

Yes. All templates are designed for square (1:1) social media posts. Export as PNG at 1080x1080 for feed posts.
